      About Skein

      I'm disabled, and every extra lap of the store costs me more than it costs most people. I built Skein to plan the shortest walk past everything on my list, and it helps anyone in a hurry too.

      The name comes from Ariadne's skein of thread, which led Theseus out of the labyrinth.

      The store layout and item locations can be off, so treat the route as a good guess and tell me what's wrong.

      Ken Chollacoop
