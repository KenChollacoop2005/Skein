Trace the store by walking it

Skein counts your steps and reads the compass while you walk, and turns the walk into a store map. It takes one trip: the outside loop, the checkout, then each aisle.

Hold the phone in front of you, screen up, pointing where you walk. Walk at your normal pace. Everything saves as you go.

Step length starts as a guess from your height. To check it, walk 10 steps along floor tiles: most store tiles are 1 foot (0.305 m) wide.

If the store isn't a plain rectangle, keep right angles on and tap Corner at each corner during the loop. Use 45 degrees only if some walls are angled.

1. Walk the outside loop

Walk along the walls all the way around the store, about an arm's length from the shelves, and come back to the exit. Tap a department when you are next to it. Tap it again at the other end of a long wall like Dairy or Meat.

2. Mark the checkout

Walk to the self checkout. Tap Mark at each spot where you would end a trip. Then tap Done.

3. Walk each aisle

Go to the front end of an aisle, check the number, and tap Start. Walk down the middle to the back end and tap End. Walk to the next aisle and repeat.

Mark where you are

Tap a department when you are next to it. If a sign shows a number, like Produce table 2 or Deli 5, type it first.
